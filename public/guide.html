  1. Open Plan & approvals → Family tasks. Add one task and choose who is responsible.
  2. Choose the audience first: Family includes the children; Parents private includes only the adults.
  3. Choose an assistant or Human only. Record the source note and whether Cyrus must approve completion.
  4. Open the saved task and select Prepare assistant handoff. Review the text, copy it, and paste into your own assistant. Nothing is sent automatically.
  5. Bring the answer and sources back. Add a completion note; mark complete after any required task approval.

For Hub coordinator tasks, Open in coordinator chat prepares the prompt in the correct room. Review it and choose Ask coordinator to send. Existing drafts are preserved. ChatGPT and consumer Copilot handoffs use your existing subscriptions manually. No chat-to-chat API, paid provider, booking or purchase is activated by assigning a task. Muse is excluded.

Approvals

Approving a task records a decision. Sending an email or creating a calendar event still requires Cyrus to review and approve that exact action under Calendar & email actions. A copied assistant prompt cannot grant permission.
